  • Illustrative Only: This calculator provides estimates based on current inputs and simplified financial models. It is not financial advice.

  • Accuracy: Calculations assume constant salary, interest rates, and loan terms. They do not account for future inflation, tax changes, or specific individual circumstances.
  • Security & Privacy: All calculations are performed entirely in your browser. No personal data or input is stored or transmitted.
