Important Disclaimers

This calculator provides approximate estimates only. UK tax bands, NI, and student loan rates may change without notice. Always consult the GOV.UK for the latest information before making decisions.

All calculations are illustrative and do not constitute financial advice, a lending offer, or a definitive tax assessment. For advice tailored to your situation, consult a qualified financial advisor.

Student loan repayments: SLC/HMRC officially calculate deductions based on your "NICable pay" (pay subject to Class 1 National Insurance). This includes reductions for salary sacrifice pension contributions if your employer participates.

  • Pension and national insurance savings reflect 2025/26 rates and bands and may differ from your payroll's final figures.
  • If you have multiple loans (UG + PG), repayments are added and calculated per current HMRC/SLC rules.
  • Interest rates may adjust annually; confirm with SLC for your official rate.
  • Results do not factor in other deductions (e.g. childcare, etc.).
